## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a peaceful, restorative digital sanctuary reminiscent of analog storybooks, slow-living journals, and tranquil indie illustrations. It is crafted for creative thinkers, mindful writers, introspective creators, and slow-tech enthusiasts who seek refuge from high-velocity, over-stimulating modern interfaces.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Organic Warmth** with **Gentle Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Expansive, breathing negative space that replicates the open air of a lakeside morning or forest glade.
- **Surface Texture:** Paper-soft canvas planes, subtle grain, and organic framing rather than harsh digital cards.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Comforting, intentional, soft-spoken, and deeply grounding. Interactions feel gentle, tactile, and non-demanding.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from atmospheric watercolor and pixelated nature vistas, balancing calm earthy greens and creamy paper backdrops with a vibrant, welcoming coral spark.

- **Primary (`#df735e` / `#e66c54`):** Warm terracotta/salmon coral. Used sparingly for key calls to action, active navigation markers, and inviting focal points.
- **Secondary (`#c3ded7` / `#d7e8e2`):** Soft mist sage and gentle seafoam. Provides tranquil environmental tints, secondary pills, and soft tint fills.
- **Tertiary & Backgrounds (`#faf7f2` / `#f4eee3`):** Warm linen and parchment tones that replace clinical pure whites, reducing glare and cultivating an analog paper feel.
- **Neutrals (`#1d332d` / `#274039`):** Deep desaturated pine and charcoal forest green. Used for all primary typographic hierarchy and structural iconography instead of black.
- **Muted Borders & Washes:** `rgba(29, 51, 45, 0.08)` to `rgba(29, 51, 45, 0.14)` for subtle sketch-like outlines that retain organic gentleness.

## Typography

The type system blends the quirky, handcrafted geometric charm of **Space Grotesk** for headlines with the warm, open, highly legible humanism of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for running copy and functional UI tags.

- **Display & Headlines:** Space Grotesk features idiosyncratic letterforms and subtle vernacular quirks that suggest indie, thoughtful craftsmanship without sacrificing digital crispness. Keep headlines concise and punctuated with intentional phrasing.
- **Body & Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans softens the reading experience, providing generous apertures and rounded terminals that maintain comfortable pacing across long reflections, notes, and journals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on an unhurried, centered cadence with generous outer margins and vertical room.

- **Layout Structure:** A fluid 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1120px` to guarantee an intimate, storybook reading measure.
- **Rhythm:** Vertical margins prioritize large rhythmic gaps (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`) between thematic clusters. Content should feel uncluttered, floating comfortably above the horizon line.
- **Breakpoints & Adaptation:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layouts, standard `1.25rem` gutters, sticky micro-navigation, and scaled display typography.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 2-column groupings with `1.5rem` gutters.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Centered narrative stages, multi-column feature groupings with `2.5rem` gutters, and scenic pictorial breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids aggressive, dark, or synthetic drop shadows in favor of natural daylight depth and soft surface tiering:

- **Surface Tiering:** Depth is established primarily through tinted ground planes (e.g., placing parchment `#faf7f2` panels or translucent pill forms over atmospheric seafoam `#c3ded7`).
- **Ambient Sunlight Shadows:** When elevation is required for interactive cards or floating controls, use an ultra-diffused, tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(29, 51, 45, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(29, 51, 45, 0.04);`
- **Soft Paper Linework:** Cards and distinct panels feature faint organic ghost strokes (`1px solid rgba(29, 51, 45, 0.08)`) to outline edges with hand-pressed precision rather than hard computational boundaries.

## Shapes

The shape language is organic, welcoming, and pill-dominant, reflecting pebbles, clouds, and handcrafted stationery:

- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** All interactive buttons, status tags, chip selectors, and primary action controls use continuous full radiuses.
- **Panel Curvature (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`):** Content enclosures, viewport containers, and modal sheets use generous radiuses (24px to 32px), eliminating sharp, stressful corners.
- **Illustrative Outlines:** Hand-rendered motifs, tree lines, and icons incorporate gently rounded caps and corners (`stroke-linejoin: round; stroke-linecap: round;`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), filled with warm coral `#df735e`, crisp cream text `#faf7f2`, and `px-6 py-3`. Hover scales gently to `transform: translateY(-1px)` with a slightly enriched coral glow.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped, tinted with translucent seafoam-white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`), pine text `#1d332d`, and an optional subtle border `rgba(29, 51, 45, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Plain pine text `#1d332d` with a rounded hover pill fill of `rgba(29, 51, 45, 0.05)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped chips with `px-4 py-1.5`, utilizing `label-sm` typography. Styled with quiet sage backdrops (`#d7e8e2`) and deep pine text `#1d332d` for tags, category filters, and tranquil status indicators.

### Cards & Sanctuary Surfaces
- Framed in `rounded-3xl`, filled with warm cream `#faf7f2` or semi-opaque mist white (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- Outlined with a faint `1px solid rgba(29, 51, 45, 0.08)` and grounded with ambient sunlight shadows.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Input Fields:** Generously padded (`py-3.5 px-5`), `rounded-2xl`, styled with a paper-cream fill `#faf7f2` and muted border. Focus transitions to a distinct yet calm pine outline (`#1d332d`) without jarring high-saturation halos.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic boxes (`rounded-md` or circular) filled with soft parchment; active states feature a terracotta `#df735e` fill with a white hand-drawn tick.

### Navigation Bar
- A floating capsule or top-anchored banner with generous whitespace, featuring an organic emblem, understated pine text links, and a persistent terracotta CTA pill.
